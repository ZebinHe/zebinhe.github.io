About

Hi! I'm Zebin He, a first-year Ph.D. student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, affiliated with the C4 Group, co-supervised by Prof. Wenhan Luo and Prof. Wei Xue.

My research interests lie in 3D vision, particularly 3D shape generation, PBR material synthesis, and 2D–3D correspondence.

Research Interests

  • PBR Material and Texture Synthesis
